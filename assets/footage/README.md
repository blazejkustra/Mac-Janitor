# Footage

Real footage, cut by `scripts/site-footage.sh`: trimmed, scaled, lightly darkened with a soft vignette, and
looped (a crossfade for the sea, forwards then backwards for the dandelion and the moon). Nothing is sped up,
upscaled or generated.

under reduced motion or Save-Data.
